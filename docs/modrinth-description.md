# Auto Totem

Tired of digging through your inventory while someone is hitting you? This mod puts a Totem of Undying into your offhand for you.

When your offhand is empty, Auto Totem grabs another totem from your inventory and swaps it in. By default it does this right away, and you can leave it at that. If you want more control, there's a settings screen (press **B**, or use Mod Menu).

## What you can tweak

- **Delay.** Wait a few ticks before the totem is equipped, if instant swaps feel too robotic.
- **Health threshold.** Off by default. Turn it on and the totem only goes into your offhand once your health falls to a number of hearts you choose, and goes back to its slot when you've recovered. There's also an option to move it right after the first hit.
- **Stats HUD.** Off by default. Shows how many totems you have left and when the last one popped.
- **Notification HUD.** A small "Totem used" message on screen.
- **Your own sound.** Pick a sound file from your computer to play when a totem is used, and set its volume. With a custom sound set, the vanilla totem sound for your own totem is muted so they don't clash.

Both HUDs can be dragged around, resized and made more or less transparent, and you can see them both on the settings screen while you set them up.

Keys: **O** toggles the mod, **B** opens settings. Rebind them in Controls.

## Things worth knowing

- Custom sounds must be WAV, AIFF or AU, up to 30 seconds. OGG and MP3 aren't supported.
- Dropping a totem with Q counts as "used", since the mod only sees the count go down.
- Some servers forbid automatic inventory actions. Check their rules.
- Does nothing in Creative or Spectator.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader, Fabric API. Mod Menu is optional. Client-side only.
